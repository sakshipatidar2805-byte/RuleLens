\# University Academic Regulations

\## Attendance Regulations



\### Section ATT-1: General Attendance Requirement



All undergraduate students are expected to maintain regular attendance throughout the academic semester. Attendance is calculated separately for each registered course and is based on the number of instructional sessions attended by the student.



A student must maintain a minimum attendance of 75 percent in a course to normally qualify for the end-semester examination. Students are responsible for monitoring their attendance through the academic portal and should report discrepancies to the course instructor within the period prescribed by the department.



Attendance recorded after the scheduled reporting period may not be considered unless the department determines that an administrative error occurred.



\### Section ATT-2: Attendance Calculation



Course attendance is calculated using the number of eligible instructional sessions attended divided by the total number of eligible instructional sessions conducted during the semester.



Approved academic activities, institutional events, and officially authorized university representation may be treated as attended sessions when supporting authorization has been recorded by the responsible department.



Students should retain documentary evidence for any attendance adjustment request.



\### Section ATT-3: Medical Absence



A student who is unable to attend classes because of a documented medical condition may submit a medical attendance request to the department.



The request should normally contain appropriate medical documentation and should be submitted within the period specified by the department after the student's return to academic activity.



Approval of medical documentation does not automatically guarantee restoration of attendance. The department must review whether the absence satisfies the conditions for an attendance concession.



\### Section ATT-4: Attendance Concession



For an approved medical circumstance, a student whose attendance is below the normal requirement may be considered for an attendance concession.



Under the medical concession provision, students may be considered eligible for examination participation when their attendance is at least 65 percent, provided that the medical circumstance has been properly documented and approved.



The concession is intended for exceptional circumstances and is not a substitute for regular attendance.



\### Section ATT-5: Attendance Shortage



A student whose attendance falls below the applicable eligibility threshold may be prevented from appearing in the end-semester examination.



Students are expected to resolve attendance concerns before the examination registration deadline. Mere submission of a representation does not establish examination eligibility.



\### Section ATT-6: Department Review



The department may review attendance records when a student submits evidence of an administrative error, approved academic activity, or documented exceptional circumstance.



Any decision affecting attendance eligibility should be recorded through the appropriate academic channel.



\### Section ATT-7: Student Responsibility



Students are responsible for regularly checking their attendance records. Ignorance of the attendance percentage shown in the academic portal does not constitute grounds for automatic exemption.



Students should communicate attendance concerns before examination registration whenever reasonably possible.



\### Section ATT-8: Attendance and Examination Registration



Examination registration does not by itself establish eligibility to sit for an examination. Eligibility remains subject to the applicable attendance requirements and any approved concession.



Where multiple provisions appear applicable to the same case, the responsible academic authority should review the relevant provisions before making a final determination.



\### Section ATT-9: Record Keeping



Departments should maintain attendance records for each course. Corrections to attendance records should be supported by an appropriate academic or administrative record.



Students may request clarification regarding recorded attendance through the official academic communication channel.



\### Section ATT-10: Scope



These provisions apply to undergraduate academic courses unless a course-specific regulation expressly establishes a different attendance procedure.



Where a course-specific regulation exists, students should consult the course regulation together with the general attendance provisions.
